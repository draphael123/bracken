HOLY FIRE: the trough's burner bed (soot lining, a brass rail with jets), BLAZING tongues (white core, pale, gold, an orange tip; sparks), the EMBER bed (cinders, breathing coals, smoke) with the IRON GRATE laid flat over it (the footing reads), the LAMP -> TROUGH FEED (an inlaid brass vein: bright dashes run to the fire while the lamp burns, a dead grey line when snuffed).
