# claude/hudslim - HUD-SLIM (Daniel 10-09)

Base claude/fontpair 092bdcf9. All in src/main.js (HUD block + boss bar), three env-row files (y +2), tools.

## What changed
1. MINIMAL HUD is the default. `SET.hud` defaults to 'minimal'; a settings file with no `hudV2` mark (every save that exists) takes MINIMAL once, then `hudV2:1` is saved and an explicit PLATE choice sticks (readSettings). Settings row reads MINIMAL / PLATE. Minimal = no plate anywhere: bars keep their 1px dark outline, text over the world is a 1px outline (HP number sits ON the bar end, coins, quest line, LV line, clock). Idle (full HP and stamina, no meter, no cooldown, no status): the bars/LV line are not drawn (they are drawn into a 1x1 NULLG context, one code path) - but the coins, clock, hint toasts, skill row, flask row and boss bar are ALWAYS on (before, idle MINIMAL also hid the hint toasts, clock and vignette: a bug that would have hit every player once it became the default).
2. Plate HUD (opt-in) is ONE slim plate, 66 px of the 320 view (20.6%; was 116-190, up to 45% with a meter prompt). Meter prompts (LAST CHARGE: C etc.) now sit on a status strip BESIDE the plate, not in it. tools/hud-default.mjs asserts <= 22% for all 7 heroes meters-full and swimming.
3. One icon scale/cell: heart 9x8, bolt (new) 7x8, flask (new HUD flask) 7x8, rows on an 8px pitch (HP y4, stamina y12, FLASK ROW y20-28, hero meter y31, XP y39), all 1 px per pixel with a dark outline. Status icons sit on strips: the caravan sun/water/frost rows (moved down 2 px, on a dark strip), venom drops (strip in the flask row), meter prompts (strip).
4. ONE BOSS BAR: name once on the plate, one bar, ONE readout to the right of the bar per the Boss health setting: BAR = none, BAR+% = "62%", BAR+NUMBERS = "900/1451". The old BAR+BOTH is gone (a saved 'both' loads as NUMBERS); the plate no longer grows an extra row for the figures. The intro name card moved from across the middle of the screen (it covered the boss's head) into the top band (13% bars, name centred in the top one).
5. HUD numbers: stayed Silkscreen at size 8 (call sites were never x2); HP number, coins, clock, LV line are drawn with a 1px dark outline over the world (the clock lost its plate for it). Plate mode keeps its plate behind the rest.
6. FLASK SLOT: the flasks are drawn in the reserved row under stamina (y20-28, x4+8i, always on, a gold mark above one over the max). FLASKS 2 builds its row there - nothing else lives in it but the venom strip (to the right).
