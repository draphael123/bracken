## Item 2 - seed after load
- src/main.js: loadLevelG's coin/silver/banner phase draws and spawnEntities (the whole spawn, incl. respawn on death) now run on a private deterministic stream (hash of the level id), swapping Math.random in and restoring it in finally. Level load no longer consumes the pinned stream, so adding/removing a decor ent no longer shifts seeded fights. (lab.js bossLab already reseeds after load; the engine now guarantees it for every test.)
- Side effect: foe/prop spawn timers and phases come from the load stream, so they are the SAME every load of a level (previously drawn from whatever Math.random was). Prod loses a little spawn-timer variety per visit; tell me if you want a per-load counter mixed in (rec: no, determinism is the point).
