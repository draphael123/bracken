## What changed

- `tools/canal-water.mjs`: knows the new shoal level.
- `docs/mash-bot.json`: the canal boss rows, re-stamped by the bot.
- `src/boss-greed.js`: untouched. OWN_WARD and OPEN_RULE (`gtOpen`) still hold. `gtOpen` now also covers STUCK and DAZED.

1. **One machine beat per phase.** A paddle is ONE timed strike. There is no worked progress bar and no knock-back.
   - **Phase 1:** strike the drain while she is at its gate (the paddle glows when it is time). She is STRANDED for 3.2 s at x1.7. She drags herself to the culvert and lets the water back in at HALF, once. A strike that is too early does nothing and costs nothing; the line is "NOT YET".
   - **Phase 2:** the lock floods and she hides in a culvert once. One strike on that gate's paddle FLUSHES her (3.2 s, x1.7). The wrong paddle does nothing. If you leave her there 9 s, she comes out with a surge, so there is no soft-lock.
   - **Phase 3:** the fog comes down and the water falls once, to a SHOAL. The sunken narrowboat's back is now a hand under the surface.
   - Every other paddle strike does nothing ("THE PADDLE IS DONE: FIGHT HER").
   - Gone: her hand on the paddle, the knots, the running upper paddle, the lamp hooks (the lamps now just hang as the fog's lights).
2. **Openings come from fighting her.** Each lasts at least 3 s at x1.25:
   - **ARM-STUCK:** step out of her SLAM on a waler or walkway and her claws stick in the timber. She hangs off the ledge's end. A blow on her body, or along the stuck arm (a new `hurtBoss` hook), lands.
   - **BITE PARRIED:** meet her yellow bite and she is DAZED at the surface. "Meet" means a shield block, the warden's deflect, the pyro's flare, or rolling through it.
   - **LURE:** in the fog's lure cycles, stand on the boat. Her charge across the shallow runs aground and she is STRANDED on the deck.
   - Stuck and dazed she does nothing (stagger means still). In the beat openings she only swipes low (!!, jump).
   - After every opening comes a told ~3 s **WARY** ward: a ring of weed, the line "SHE IS WARY: NOT THE SAME TRICK TWICE", and she will not throw the move that opened her.
   - The cycle turns on each opening: a new weed pattern and a new move she leans on. The lure is on, then off, in the fog.
3. **More varied moves, one new per phase.**
   - Phase 1: the SLAM (!!, step out).
   - Phase 2: the SUBMERGED CHARGE, a bow-wave along the lock (!!, jump).
   - Phase 3: the WEED NET (!!, step out). If it catches you, you are rooted 0.9 s and her next move comes 0.3 s sooner.
   - Paired arms, and the fog's double grabs, are kept.
   - She now has a deck of moves per lean, instead of a strict rotation.
   - Her arms reach from her (170 px), so she no longer lashes across the whole lock.
   - In the fog she drags herself slowly over the boat (arms busy) instead of being stuck on one side of it.
4. **Sprite redrawn at native size, 76x64, no 1.6x upscale.**
   - A gaunt hag: a weed cap and fringe, sunk glowing eyes, a hooked nose, green teeth, long dripping hair with duckweed, long knuckly arms, sodden sacking.
   - 19 frames, with a distinct pose for every told move: slam (risen, arms high), stuck (hanging), dazed (tongue out), charge (pitched forward, hair streaming), net (arm back with the weed), and the net thrown.
   - Her body box is 30x46, matching the art (sheet: `node tools/greenteeth-sheet.mjs`).
   - **Also fixed:** the game's swimmer wash (`drawSwimmers`) was repainting her whole sprite pale. That is a big part of why she looked "odd". She is now exempt (a one-line hook in `main.js`), so she is crisp above the water and under the lock's green below it.
   - Stills: `work/claude/jenny2/*.png` (from `tools/greenteeth-shots.mjs`).
