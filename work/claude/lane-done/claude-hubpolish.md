2. SWAP HERO (new src/hero-swap.js + main.js): one panel with all nine portraits (owned lit with level + first equipped skill icon; locked greyed with a padlock and the unlock line from the store's own locks, e.g. "BUY HIM AT THE STORE: 10 SILVER", "BEAT THE ... TO EARN IT (OR 10 SILVER)"). F / pad throw / tap on SWAP HERO in the store; H on the map and on the store's SKILLS tab; pause menu "Swap hero" (only on the map, in a shop room, in the inn); LEFT/RIGHT walk, confirm takes him, ESC closes; tap a cell (tap again to take). A swap uses the store's own path (setEquip + applySkin + applyUpgrades + save), so each hero keeps his own level/skills/charm. A hero another co-op seat plays is refused. TAVERN: the wardrobe card gets a roster strip (every hero, the seat's choices lit, current marked) beside the existing LEFT/RIGHT on the HERO row (one press already swapped; the strip shows who you can reach).
3. STORES WITH LIFE (new src/shop-life.js): in all four walk-in shops - keeper idle loops (polish a mug / count coins / ledger + quill), looks up with a "!" and greets when you come in, rotating bark lines (2 greets, 5 barks, 3 thanks per shop, shop-specific voice), paw-up frame while speaking; hanging sign swaying on chains with a per-shop emblem (coin/pick/anchor/bucket); shop-specific wares on the counter and hung from the wall (herbs+sacks / picks+lanterns / rope+brass / cloth+jugs); kettle with steam; a critter (cat; raven on the High Store sign; parrot on the Chandler's sign; gecko at the Well); dust motes; flickering lamplight (stacked discs, no gradient). Store SCREEN: keeper head at the foot of the page that nods on a purchase, a bark line every ~11 s in place of the help text, candle flames in the panel corners, and a purchase beat (coin clink = the game's own, ring + sparks round the item, keeper says a buy line; back in the room he nods and says thanks). Tavern: dust in the firelight and a barkeep line (5 lines) when you are near the bar; the room's fire/lanterns/window/drinkers were already animated in tavern_backdrop.js.
   No canvas, gradient or array is made per frame in the room (hero-swap tool measures 0 new canvases over 240 frames in each of the four rooms; update cost 0.1-0.3 ms).
