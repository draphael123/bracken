## What was built
**The foe - GOBLIN GEOMANCER (`gobgeo`)**, the Queen's court earth-mage: purple-and-gold robe and hood, the Queen's circlet, a long hooked nose and a white beard, a stone staff with an amethyst geode, three slate chunks orbiting him.
- `src/gob-geomancer.js` (pure rules, numbers `GG`), `src/gobgeo-hands.js` (binds it to the game), `src/redraw/gob_geomancer_art.js` (15 frames: idle x2, walk x4, rock tell, throw, slate tell, slate sweep, shell tell, shell burst, stagger, death heap, hurt - outline, 5-tone ramps lit by the normal, round volumes; the Frog King bar).
- Kit, every move told with a yellow `!` (marks.js BY_HAND / ANSWER / HEIGHT, MARK regenerated):
  - **ROCK LOB** `rockTell` 0.75 s (staff up, a chunk rises over his head, glowing) -> an arcing rock, 11.
  - **SLATE VOLLEY** every third throw, `slateTell` 0.9 s (staff planted, slates tear out of the floor) -> 3 flat slates in a fan, 8 each (the Queen's slate shards, small).
  - **SHELL BURST** close (< 52 px) `shellTell` 0.5 s -> his chunks burst outward, 8 + a shove; then his orbit is empty and he is SPENT 1.4 s (x1.5).
  - **Guard / weak spot**: while stone orbits him, a blow into his FRONT is WARDED (x0.35, knocks a chunk away; never untouchable). Back, from above, empty orbit, and his own rock sent back are whole. Strike him while the rock is over his head and it falls on HIM (+6, staggered 1 s).
  - Keeps 90-170 px, never steps off his ledge, leashed to his post, no contact damage.
- **SENT BACK** (the Queen's verb): a rock/slate the hero PARRIES (the shared parry counter moving during the hit - gqHit's rule, so every hero's parry/deflect/ward counts) or MEETS WITH A BLOW (attack box on the rock; a ranger arrow in flight too) flies straight back at its thrower and smashes the FIRST thing in its path: a rocklock (seal / old stone), a gate bell (Stormhold's src/bell-gate.js: its guards run to it, the gate drops on them), a chandelier (falls; a 36 px crash for 20 on goblins under it), a shield goblin from the front (SHIELD KNOCKED ASIDE = off balance 2.4 s, no damage), any other goblin (14, tagged `reflect`), and him (max(14, 36% of his bar), STAGGERED 1.4 s, orbit dropped). Walls stop it. A plain block just breaks it; a roll lets it fly on. His stone lives in its own array (not `seeds`), as the Queen's shards do.
- Registry: main.js (import, EHP 44, DMG rows, spawn, `take(e, dmg, tag)`, update branch, frame, SPR + HAS_HURT, corpse = death heap, bestiary card, COVERED (the shield wall plants in front of him), GOBLINISH, the `shooter` family for poise, contact skip), threat.js 3.5, level-quality ROLES ranged, goblin-lint GOBLIN_KINDS, check.mjs list (+gobgeo). All main.js edits are single tagged lines `(claude/gobgeo)`.
