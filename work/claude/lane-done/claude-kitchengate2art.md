## WHAT WAS DRAWN (same hook functions and footprints as the greybox)
- **Kitchen dressing (drawProp, all 8 kinds, replaces the 25 placeholders):** brick bread oven (dome, open iron door, fire glow, flue + smoke, peel), spice rack (labelled glass jars, garlic braid, dried herbs), flour sacks (stitched, tied necks, spilled dust, scoop), butcher block (end-grain top, iron hoop, cleaver, blood, rolled apron), steam vent (slotted floor grate, flange, rolling steam), scullery sink (stone, copper tap + drip, plate stack, drainer), serving hatch (brick surround, half-down shutter, lit mouth, brass bell, plated roast), hanging pot rack (copper pans, skillet, ladle).
- **Serving line (drawLine):** tarred-canvas belt with wooden cleats that run with the scroll, spoked turning end rollers, steel rails, gilt chevrons the way it runs; the counter variant is glazed green tile on iron legs with foot-plates.
- **Stove flares (drawFlare):** cast-iron griddle plate (rolled lip, grease grooves, rivets, end stops); the glow tell = red under-glow, rising sparks, a flicker; then the flame sheet over a hot plate.
- **Meat-hook rail (drawMeatRail):** roller chain running along the rail, trolleys on S-hooks, with a pig-side, a gammon or a sausage string on each.
- **ATTENDANT (7 frames, bakeAttendant):** plum livery coat, gold frogging + epaulette, white cravat and apron, white gloves, bald pate, side whiskers, waxed moustache, white stockings, buckled shoes. Idle/walk carry a tray with a silver cloche (reads as a waiter, not a blade); aim = plate cocked with gravy; throw; guard = the tray as a raised silver disc with a gilt boss; bash. China plate with a blue rim in flight (drawPlate). Butcher: kept as the greybox human reskin (skin, beard, no tusk).
- **LORD GRUBBAG:** every pose (the seated model from claude/grubbagbig: lobTell/lob, flingTell/fling, belchTell/belch, sweepTell/sweep, greaseTell/grease, bellTell/bell, grabTell/grab, eat, gulp, stun, hot, recover, sleep/wake) was already drawn on his full seated model by the greybox; checked on the new pose sheet: no fork, no tureen, nothing clips the chair, every hand stays off the floor line (grab/sweep arms reach out by design). Added: STUN = soot on face + singed spikes on the circlet + stars wheeling round his head (with the existing smoke); HOT keeps the red flush and ear steam. Not redrawn: the arm model (see Q2).
- **His hall (grubbag_art.js):** kitchen hatch (brick arch, roller shutter, ladle rack against the fire glow, steam, riveted iron chute); serving belt (green tile); his platter / table end (plum cloth with gold swags, silver charger, fork, cloche pushed aside); grease cauldron (riveted iron, bail lug, amber grease skin, alternating-link chain, pull-ring) + the chain to his fist; burning floor (ragged 3-layer flames on a molten bed, sparks, glow); PEPPER STOVE = the old pie oven dressed with red-pepper strings, a chilli sign, a red glaze band and an ember glow when hot.
- **Food / bomb / chilli sprites (dish):** roast dinner (glazed gammon, bone, roast potatoes, parsley); BOMB = a black steamed pudding with custard drizzle, holly and a lit fuse; CHILLI = a fat glossy red-hot chilli with green stalk, glowing plate, heat shimmer; ham in flight (glazed gammon, bone knuckle, scoring); flat plate (rim, gravy, speed streaks).
