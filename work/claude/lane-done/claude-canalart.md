# claude/canalart - THE FOG CANAL: the art pass

Base: master 9138e240 (the canal and Jenny Greenteeth live). Layout, geometry, tuning and foe timing are untouched. One level-data line was added: `bgSpan: 200` in src/fog-canal.js (the backdrop rides within 200 px whatever storey the camera is on, so the summit is not off the bottom of it). Pictures: work/claude/canalart/before and /after (tools/canalart-shots.mjs, one set each).

## What changed
- OWN BACKDROP (src/redraw/canal_backdrop.js; main.js stops borrowing the `town` layers for the canal). Far: misty fields, poplars, distant mills with turning sails, Waymeet's steeple behind with lit windows. Mid: towpath hedges, lock cottages with a lit window, moored narrowboats (a stern lantern, a painted stripe), a brick warehouse back with its hoist. Near: reeds, mooring posts with a rope ring, hung lanterns (amber, the only warm light up front, drawn faint so it never reads as footing). Soft mist banks drift at each layer's pace and every layer ends in a mist fade, not a hard line. Night blue-grey, lantern amber.
- TILE KIT (src/redraw/canal_tiles.js, one hook in main.js resolveTiles): wet red-brown brick quays, a lit stone coping on every quay edge, a green algae line at every water level a lock can stand at, black tarred lock gates with a white-painted top end, towpath boards on corbels, swing-bridge decks, Jenny's chamber in slimed green stone (plus her own lock skins used before the fight starts, so the chamber is dressed on arrival).
- SOFT FOG (canalhands drawCanalFog -> canal_props featherBank): each bank draws with feathered, billowing left/right edges, a soft top, puffs drifting through it, and a scalloped lip on the thick ones. Feathering is inward only: the covered extents (the gameplay) are identical.
- THE BARGE (src/redraw/canal_props.js): a painted narrowboat, tarred hull, rust strake, a low cabin at the bow with roses and castles, a lantern pole with a caged lantern at the stern. THE TILLER is now visible at the STERN: a brass-headed rudder post and a long bar that sweeps up (the mill cut / offside) or down (the weir / towpath) to a brass grip amidships, with a clear UP or DOWN arrow over it. The strike point is the same grip where it always was (see Q1).
- OFFSIDE vs TOWPATH: on the offside she is hazed (a blue-grey wash on the hull), has a bow wave and a wake, and the towpath's shade lies across the near water. Her hit boxes are not moved.
- LOCK MACHINES: balance beams with a white end over each shut gate (a stub when open), seeping water; the paddle is a rack and a wheel (the rack is up when the paddle is, the wheel green when the chamber fills); swing bridges have a white rail and an iron pivot drum; capstans have bars (amber across, green swung); the foghorn is brass with a bellows box and its wind-up gauge; lantern posts are iron with a caged lantern and a pool of warm light (doused: a thread of smoke); booms are chained logs; the sill, the skiff and the child's buckle shoe are drawn properly. Bright weed is springy green with pale tips and bubbles; dark weed is a flat dull skin.
- WATER: a sheen on each pound and lantern reflections in the water (amber streaks under the barge's lantern and every lit post; a cold green shimmer under a wisp).
- FOES (src/redraw/canal_foes_art.js; same frames, anchors, boxes): the GRINDYLOW is redrawn with a pale teal rim light (a dark-green imp on near-black water was a hole), long thin arms, weed hair, needle teeth and big pale eyes; the WISP is a cold green white-cored flame with a faint face and trailing flecks, and on top of the fog it is a cold teardrop with no cage, no post, a flare ring in its tell, whereas every real lantern is warm amber, square-caged on a post, steady; the LAMPLIGHTER (the snuffer's frames) wears a dark-blue long coat with brass buttons and a flat cap, carries a pole-lantern with a real cage, and his lantern lights him.
- ROOMS: the warehouse (brick, timber frame, moonlit window, crates, sacks, a hoist hook), the mill (planks, millstones, turning gear wheels, hung sacks), Jenny's door passage (barred, wet) - all with a lit lantern.
- JENNY'S LOCK: weed in curtains from the gate walkways, slime sliding down the wall faces, and the sunken narrowboat lair (ribs, a torn tarp, a snapped tiller, two cold-green eyes blinking in its hatch). Her sprite is NOT touched: it is clearly not a placeholder (greenteeth_art.js).

## UNVERIFIED
- No pilot was run (nothing moved). The canal check's page tests did run and pass.
- Looked at in god-mode stills only, not played. The fog is still heavy over much of the level, so the backdrop mostly shows where the fog is thin or cleared (a horn). Worth a play on a normal monitor.
- Mid-layer height was tuned at the pound, the mill pound and the basin; the summit (row 18) and the weir race were not individually checked.
- The barge hull below the waterline is hidden by the water by design; her cabin is drawn at the bow ABOVE the deck, behind the hero.
- The near layer (reeds, posts) is deliberately faint (55 percent) so it cannot read as footing; shout if it still does.
