# Level 14 — five pitches

The arc so far: forest → marsh → goblin camp → fungus → court → foothills → cliff town → crystal mountain →
moor → last hold → castle → river → reef → flotilla. The sea arc ends with the Quartermaster's flagship, so
14 either lands the fleet somewhere or turns inland with what the sea gave you.

## 1. THE SALT WORKS  (the fleet's larder)  ★ my pick
Terraced evaporation pans on a white coast: shallow, waist-deep brine that the tide floods pan by pan on a
timer you can read from the sluices. Verbs it already has: swimming, the sluice gates from The Long Water,
the swell from the Flotilla. New rule: **the pans fill in sequence**, so a route that walks now swims in
twenty seconds, and the salt crust over a drained pan is a SHELF that gives way. Foes: salt-burned labourers,
a brine-witch who opens the gates early, rakers with long-handled tools that outrange you. Boss: **THE
PANMASTER**, who fights you across three pans and floods the one you are standing in.

## 2. THE DROWNED TOWN  (what the Herald's tide took)
A town under twenty feet of water with its roofs and steeple above the surface: above the water it is a
platformer, below it is the swimming level, and the two halves are the same map. Air bells in the chimneys.
Every door is a choice of route. Boss: something that lives in the flooded church and hates the light.
Strongest theme, the most work — it needs the whole town drawn twice.

## 3. THE WRECKERS' COAST  (lanterns that lie)
Cliffs above a shore of broken ships. The wreckers move false lights to put ships on the rocks; you can move
them back. Set piece: a ship comes in through the dark and where it lands is up to you — one lantern position
opens a route across her masts, another drops her on the rocks and gives you salvage instead. Boss: **THE
LAMPLIGHTER** on the cliff head, fought around the light itself (the beam is an attack and a platform).

## 4. THE WHALE ROAD  (a level on one animal)
A bone-white leviathan hunted by the fleet's whaleboats; you cross it while they harpoon it. The ground
breathes, rolls and dives — a moving level with a clock. The dive is the level's timer and the harpoon lines
are the ropes. Boss: the harpoon crew's **IRONWIFE** on the beast's back. Very strong, technically the riskiest.

## 5. THE LOCKS  (inland, on stolen water)
Turn inland: a canal staircase climbing away from the sea, the Queen's engineers running the locks. Water
levels rise and fall as a puzzle you drive, boats as moving platforms, and the gates from Highcrown as keys.
Pairs well as the bridge between the sea arc and whatever ends the game.

## What 14 needs regardless of pitch
- One rule the player can read from across the room (the pans filling, the tide, the beam, the dive).
- A mid-level fight that is not the boss.
- Its own music (CC0), its own creature voices, a TIER entry, a map node, a checkpoint before the boss.
