MAIN ROAD: Stormhold -> THE KITCHEN GATE -> Highcrown (re-point crown's `needs`; map node between). You sneak into the
goblin queen's castle the back way: in with the supply carts (the Ore Road is "the castle's supply line") and up
through the lower ward's kitchens and feast hall to her court. Interior castle palette: a step from walls to halls.
MECHANICS (each required; exam combines): (1) ROTATING SPITS - huge roasting spits as turning platforms over fire pits;
(2) MEAT-HOOK RAILS - ride hanging hooks across the ceiling; (3) GREASE + STEAM - greased floors you slide on, cauldron
steam that lifts you.
FOES: goblins REUSED as cooks/servers; ranged = a RESKINNED goblin thrower lobbing pots and cleavers; ONE NEW = the GOBLIN
BUTCHER (a heavy cleaver brute). Role mix >= 3.
SET PIECE: an overturned grease pot sets the kitchen alight; race the fire along the grease trails (agency: routes).
COLLECTIBLE: the head cook's RECIPE PAGES -> the SPICE VAULT (relic + silver).
BOSS: LORD GRUBBAG THE GLUTTON - the queen's glutton of a brother; NEVER LEAVES HIS REINFORCED CHAIR. Servers run dishes
to him on serving lines; EVERY DISH HEALS HIM / POWERS HIS NEXT ATTACK. Attacks from the chair: BELCH (floor shockwave,
jump), THROWN FOOD (drumsticks/bones, arcing), TABLE SLAM (planks/plates fly), GIANT FORK (long stab up close), GRAVY

OPENING, >= 3 s). Phases: greedier, faster servers; last phase he reaches out of the chair to GRAB you as food.
DISTINCT FROM KING GORM (Kingswood, a fat king who FIGHTS) - Grubbag only eats.
