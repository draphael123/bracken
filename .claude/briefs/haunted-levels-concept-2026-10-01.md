## THE HOLLOW MANOR (haunted house)
MECHANICS: (1) POSSESSED ARMOUR - an empty suit only fights while a ghost is inside; knock the ghost out and the suit
drops (and can be re-possessed); (2) PHASING GHOSTS through walls/floors; (3) ROOMS THAT REARRANGE WHEN UNWATCHED
(builds on the fair's facing rule). Lights going out are a threat.
SET PIECE: the lights go out room by room; carry a CANDELABRA through a chase by the ghosts (agency: route, which
candles to relight).
FOES: haunts/boos/wights REUSED; possessed armour = RESKINNED knight AIs; ONE NEW: a POLTERGEIST that throws furniture
(the ranged foe).
COLLECTIBLES: PORTRAIT KEYS (each family portrait) unlock the SEALED NURSERY = relic + silver.
BOSS: THE CASTELLAN'S GHOST - possesses the armoury, ONE SET AT A TIME, each its own fight (~25-30 s each):
  1 KNIGHT (sword + shield: blocks, shield bash, told thrust); 2 HALBERD (long sweeps, reach: jump/duck);
  3 DUELIST (rapier + dagger: fast feints, lunges - a dodge test); 4 GREATSWORD (slow, huge, armour-breaking arcs).
  Break a set -> he flees to the next rack. FINAL PHASE: he swaps bodies MID-COMBO between the remaining racks.
  Openings per set earned by its moveset (e.g. the halberd overextends); readable which set is live.

## THE PLAGUE VILLAGE
MECHANICS: (1) THE BELL FLIPS THE WORLD - ruin <-> the night it died (doors, floors, foes differ in each; use the flip to
cross); (2) BODYCARTS and PLAGUE PITS; (3) a WARD of herbs/lime that keeps ghosts off (carried / laid).
SET PIECE: the DEATH-CART PROCESSION you follow through the past world (agency: keep pace, choose when to flip).
FOES: reuse (haunts, drowned/wights reskinned as villagers, rats as swarms) + at most ONE new; a ranged foe (e.g. a
reskinned thrower).
COLLECTIBLES: the villagers' NAMES (grave markers) unlock the SEXTON'S HUT = relic + silver.
BOSS: THE BANSHEE - her WAIL is the tell (screams shatter platforms + stun); she drifts between worlds on the bell;
SILENCE IS THE OPENING - ring the bell mid-wail to cut her scream (>= 3 s, full damage).
