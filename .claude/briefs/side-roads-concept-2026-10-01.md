# THE SIDE ROADS - CONCEPT CONFIRM (Daniel interview 2026-10-01; bookings + bosses from 2026-09-28 in HANDOFF)
RULE FOR ALL (incl. THE MINECART - see the-minecart-concept.md): a downloaded CC0 level track each (Daniel's go first,
one batch list), synth boss themes; max 1 new foe type each (Sky Road: ZERO); Opus greybox -> reviewer vs Mage's Folly
-> fixes -> Sonnet art; level-quality gated; each mechanic required, exam combines, set piece with agency, ranged foe,
role mix >= 3, collectibles unlock something (HUD says what), checkpoints ~1 per 200 route tiles.

THE ROCKSLIDE (off the Scree Path) - boss THE AVALANCHE KING (stone sledge ahead of the slide, told boulders; bait his
sledge into a crag; P2 the avalanche catches up). A CHASE, NOT an autoscroller: the avalanche pushes at a set pace,
out-runnable, 2-3 calm caves/overhangs between stretches. Mechanics: SCREE SLOPES (slide, hard to stop), the
AVALANCHE CHASE, BOULDERS you trigger to crush foes / block the slide's path. Ranged: goblin SLINGERS on crag tops
(reskin). Collectible: CRAG-GOAT BELLS -> a hidden cave (relic + silver).

THE SKY ROAD (off Gale Moor) - boss THE ROC (her bench code in main.js, adapted to a gliding fight; the Roc is NOT the
Red Gorge boss). IN THE AIR, distinct from Gale Moor: GLIDING is the main verb (updrafts up, dives down, landing on rock
stacks and nests), kite lines / zip lines, vertical climbs above the cloud line, gold dawn over clouds; no ground
gust-bracing. Foes: CRAG HARPY with an AIR RULE (a blocked dive tumbles her onto the nearest stack, stunned = stomp),
FLEDGLINGS guard nests, flocks of 2-3 take turns, an ELITE harpy variant ~1 per section. No new type. Collectible:
lane recommends (not feathers - the desert L3 uses feathers); must unlock something.

THE SIEGE NIGHT (off Stormhold) - boss THE SIEGE TOWER (barrels onto its wheels, drop on its deck; P2 it reaches the
wall, fight the war-chief on top as it burns). Mechanics: BARRELS rolled/dropped onto rams and ladders; LADDERS goblins
raise that you push off (timed); FIRE ARROWS set wall sections burning (routes change). Ranged: goblin FIRE-ARCHERS
below (existing archer, fire arrows). Collectible: BANNERS rescued from the wall -> the ARMOURY (relic + silver).
Needs the barrels engine.

THE GOBLIN WARREN (under Stockade / Burning Village) - boss THE DRUM KING (rhythm fight: guards attack on the beat, break
his drum skins to throw him off-beat, P2 faster tempo). Mechanics: tunnels that dig/collapse; the goblin DRUM BEAT that
makes guards act on the beat (pre-teaches the boss); traps. Ranged: goblin archers. Collectible: MUSHROOM keys -> the
HOARD (relic + silver).

THE SEWERS (under the Lamplit Street) - boss THE RAT KING (splits into swarms; open a sluice to wash them apart and expose
the core). Mechanics: WATER LEVELS raised/lowered by sluice; RAT SWARMS you wash away; dark grates and lamps. Ranged:
crossbow CUTTHROATS (reskin). Collectible: lost KEYS -> the SMUGGLERS' VAULT (relic + silver).
