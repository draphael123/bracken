# BRACKEN — what's new

Newest first. Play it at https://bracken-nine.vercel.app

## 30 September 2026
- **One store.** The equip board, the three shop rooms and the skills screen are one store with tabs (HEROES, SKINS, WEAPONS, CHARMS,
  SKILLS, SMITH, MUSIC, PRACTICE), reached from the map (V, or Q for skills), the pause menu (Store, Skills) and every shop keeper. TAB / E
  and Q (LB / RB) change tab. It stays shut in a fight; buying still wants the map, a shop room or a lit shrine. Nothing costs more,
  and every lock is still its own level, medal or feat.

## 28 September 2026
- **Sound Test** on the main menu: every song you have heard in play unlocks there (the rest show as ???), with its credit;
  every sound effect is there from the start.

## 27 September 2026 (evening)
- **Stormhold, rebuilt:** longer, with tall watchtowers to climb for their keys, walkways between them, sentries who ring
  alarm bells, and an exam at the bridgehead before the Queen's Lance.
- **The Gate Gargoyle is stone:** only a stomp hurts him, and only after you bait him into crashing through a platform onto
  the spikes. He breathes fire now. The Battlements are twice as long, over spikes, with winds that carry you back up.
- **The Burial Caverns:** the Buried Dead has two new attacks (Grave Hands and Grave Breath) and lost his crown; the Drowned
  Ossuary has sinking biers, grasping hands and a hidden high walkway; stone ledges replace the mine boards.
- **The Falling Tower:** the Undead Archmage fights at the top of the tower under the moon, with a decoy ring and a ring
  trap; far fewer flyers over the climbing; the Sexton's bell pit is spiked. Ten boss fights now stay zoomed out.
- **Gale Moor and the Scree Path:** gusts you can read, a quarry exam, and the Ram Lord can be baited into the scree bank.
- **Fewer talkers:** most NPCs are gone; quest relics are now pickups where the quest items used to be.
- Fixed: the desert gate at the end of the Falling Tower no longer draws see-through.

## 27 September 2026
- **The Burial Caverns, reworked:** shorter and no longer repetitive - five places each with their own backdrop, gas vents you
  have to time, barrow soldiers, and **the Buried Dead now opens up because of something you do**, not on a timer.
- **The Witchlight Stair - the Battlements:** a new wall-walk section before the Gate Gargoyle, with **Gargoyle Whelps** that
  sit as stone, screech, and swoop at you (guard them and they clang off, dazed). **The Gate Gargoyle now summons whelps**
  instead of demons.
- **Stamina for every hero:** it runs out sooner and comes back quickly.
- Behind the scenes: the level design guide, and fixes to the test bots.

## 26 September 2026
**Fixes from playtesting**
- **The Deep can be finished.** The sunk tribute ship's hatches no longer blow you back up, and its masts no longer block the way down.
- **The Kraken:** his tentacles can no longer get stuck in platforms; his ink is switched off while it's redesigned.
- **The Death Knight** (the Unburied Field's boss) is tougher.
- **The Mage's Folly brooms** give up the chase after a few seconds and no longer fly through platforms.
- **The Geomancer's spells** have their own stone icons, and **the store shows every hero's ability icons**.
- Signs in Marsh Wood and Underleaf now point at the right things.

**New**
- **The Unburied Field:** Night on Bald Mountain all the way through; broken bridges over a ravine under arrow volleys; and
  **the Death Knight himself is the boss** - beat him to unlock him in the shop. (The old scythe-wielder waits for another level.)
- **The Drowned Causeway:** the Kraken reworked - the tide rises through the fight, cut arms grow back, he hits harder and drags
  you toward the sea. Its mini-boss is gone.
- **The Gate Gargoyle** (the Witchlight Stair): a lower room seen zoomed out, a bigger gargoyle, and he smashes through the slab
  you leave late - and crashes to the floor, stunned.
- **The Sunken Caravan:** sandstone and ruin-stone ledges instead of wood; walking uphill on dunes is slower.
- **The camera** shows more of the level and less of the ground; deep ground fades to dark.
- **Twelve new music tracks** (all CC0): the Burning Village and the Pyromancer, every mini-boss, the Monastery and its Golem,
  Gale Moor and the Windcaller, the Hanging Village, Sporewood, the Sunken Caravan, the Dune Worm and the Queen's Lance.

## 25 September 2026
- **The Deep:** a sunk tribute ship to climb down through, a cargo hold, a galley and a gun deck, drowned knights; **the Diving
  Bell** vents only when you drop a stone on his crown valve, sends his prise after your stone, and cracks out of his shell.
- **The Underwater Keep:** drowned knights and their Captain, whirlpools you shut with a lever, two dry halls, a castle look for
  every section, and checkpoints that light as you swim over them.
- **The Falling Tower:** failing stone that counts down and falls, the Sexton, the Orrery Pit - then a second pass: the Sexton no
  longer walks into walls, the tower's tiles and windows fit, the last portal opens onto the desert, and **the Undead Archmage
  fights with portals** (dodge through his ring to catch him open).
- **The Shipwreck Reef:** a half-sunk wreck with a capstan, a bell-pool, its own quest - and **the Reefmaw comes ashore** when the
  tide goes out.
- **The Long Water:** the Linn's stepping stones, the Bore with its warning, a river of fresh water.
- **The Sunken Caravan:** climbable ruined towers, a harsher sun, and bandits instead of goblins.
- **Gale Moor:** the wind is a real mechanic - ride the gusts, brace against them (crouch braces for every hero).
- **The Burning Village:** rooftop routes, a bucket and a well, a barn ambush.
- **Highcrown:** the Goblin Queen breaks her own pillars when she charges into them; invisible ledges are gone.
- **The Queen's Lance:** slower, with lookouts and archers who answer his horn.
- **The Geomancer:** an earth-shield ward, and Fault Line hits hardest at its end.
- **Side roads:** walk onto a hero's side road at its junction; beat the level before it in its silver time to open it.
