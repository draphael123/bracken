## The 2-3 worst specifics, named, for the bottom of the table

1. **THE STORE / THE CHANDLER / THE HIGH STORE** (all three shop interiors): no `far`/`mid` layer at all — a flat
   colour wall, a shelf, a counter, two lamps. Still visual-audit.md's #1 worst, unchanged. **Fix: `bar.md` item 1+2.**
2. **HIGHCROWN**: col 468-854 is grey stone with nothing on it (banners only near the very end); the redress art for
   a real great hall (`src/redraw/redress2.js`'s `castle` theme — granite wall, arched windows, plum banners,
   columns, torches, tapestries) is already painted and sitting unwired. **This is the single highest-value, lowest-risk
   fix in the game**: no new art, just wiring (see `lanes.md` #1).
3. **THE UNDERCROWN**: same story — `redress2.js`'s `undercrown` theme (foundation arches, roots, shoring, lamps)
   exists and is unwired.
4. **THE ORE ROAD (rework in flight)**: whoever lands the rework should pull the chroma up (rule 4, target 7+) and
   warm the lamps before it merges — the structural gain Daniel is seeing is real, but the level still fails the
   art-direction rules on colour, worse than most of the "weak 13".
5. **THE HANGING VILLAGE (rework in flight)**: needs the valley to show past the cliff face on the open side of each
   tier, or "a town on a cliff" keeps reading as "a town in a cave" (lookfeel review's own #1 finding on it, still
   true against this pass's capture at col 44).
6. **GALE MOOR**: still wearing the Scree Path's sky two levels back. A five-minute F6-thumbnail check would have
   caught this; add it to the redress checklist in `bar.md`.

## What's actually good (don't touch, or touch carefully)

The Flotilla, the Long Water, Bracken Wood and Kingswood are the real top tier — own ground, real mid-ground, warm
accents. Any "bring everything up to the bar" pass should use THESE four as the reference screens, not the Ore Road.
