Baseline: **master `18b5d38`** (THE PYROMANCER IS PULLED BACK: jet no longer stuns, one fireball
at a time, no plunge contact damage). Every number below describes that commit. Levels 1-3, the

## Method

- **Boss lab** (`tools/headless.mjs boss <levels>`, headless Chrome, `PORT=5999`) — every arena
  boss (23) and every mini (9) x all 6 heroes (knight, warden, pyro, paladin, pirate, reaper),
  `maxSecs: 150`.
- **Reps: 3** per (boss, hero) pair for arena bosses and minis. `bossLab` has no built-in rep
  loop, so this meant three full passes. A single pass showed real run-to-run swing (e.g.
  wood/queen/knight: 18.5s, 22.8s, 54.1s) because boss attack choice is `Math.random()`-driven,
  not seeded — so 1 rep would have been misleading. 3 reps is the brief's stated fallback for a
  cheap survey; treat single-digit-second differences between heroes as noise, and differences
  under ~30% as inconclusive. A true 21-run pass (per the balance-sample-size lesson) would cost
  roughly 7x this run and wasn't justified for a data pass.
- **`tools/curve.mjs`** — static per-level difficulty index across the whole campaign (unaffected
  by RNG; single run is authoritative for what it measures).
- **Level playtest bot** (`BK.playtest({mode:'play'})`) for the four running levels named in the
  brief — wood, marsh, stockade, fields — x all 6 heroes, single pass each (this is bot pathing +
  static level content, not boss-style RNG combat, so 1 pass is adequate).
- Full campaign roster confirmed from `src/level.js` `LEVELS` (33 entries; 23 real campaign levels
  once shop/trial/custom rooms are excluded, matching memory's "~23 levels"). Four source
  functions in `level.js` (`theHunt`, `quarryPass`, `theFrostfell`, `theSkyShip`) are **not**
  wired into `LEVELS` — unfinished/unused content, not surveyed.

## Lab-gap bosses — flag before anything else

Four fights read as **0% kill rate for all six heroes**, but the pattern says "the bot can't play
this," not "no hero can win it": zero variance rep-to-rep, and either zero engagement or identical
chip damage regardless of hero.

- **Chief (Stockade)** — every hero: `ALIVE 100%`, `taken/min 0`, in all 3 reps. Traced by hand:
  the bot's player literally stops moving (`vx: 0`) a fixed distance from the boss and never
  swings once in 150 simulated seconds. Not a real fight measurement.
- **Mother (Sporewood)** — every hero: `ALIVE 100%` but `taken/min ~254-265` (heavy, identical
  damage regardless of hero/rep). Matches the level's own rule ("the caps put you to sleep, clear
  the air before you fight") — a hazard-management step the generic bot doesn't know to do.
- **Herald (Long Water)** — every hero: `ALIVE 99-100%`, `taken/min` locked at 34-39 in every
  single run. "The tide decides where the floor is" — a swim/timing mechanic the bot can't play.
- **The Facet / golem mini (Monastery)** — every hero `ALIVE 100%` except pyro, who shows a
  nonsensical **negative** hp-left (-44% to -53%) while still flagged alive — a scoring bug in the
  mini-boss harness for this one boss, not usable data either way.

**Recommendation: someone needs to hand-play these four before Phase 2 touches them.** They are
excluded from every ranking below and must not be read as "unbeatable."

## Top of report: heroes who cannot beat a boss (real fights, not lab gaps)

- **Pirate cannot beat The Queen's Lance (Stormhold)** — 0/3 kills across reps, stalls at 25-32%
  boss HP every time, `taken/min 0` (meaning the pirate isn't dying either — this is a pure
  damage-output gap, not a survival problem).
- **The Quartermaster (The Flotilla) beats 5 of 6 heroes outright** — warden, pyro, paladin,
  pirate and reaper are 0/3; even the knight (the only hero who ever wins) is only 1/3. Real
  engagement (boss HP left varies 25-47% by hero/rep), so this reads as "too hard / too long,"
  not a lab gap.
- **The Tollmaster (The Lamplit Street) beats 5 of 6 heroes outright** — knight, warden, pyro,
  paladin and reaper are 0/3, stalling at 5-28% boss HP at the 150s cap; only pirate closes it
  (2/3, ~24s when it lands). Real engagement, same conclusion as Quartermaster.
- Secondary concern: **warden (2/3) and paladin (1/3) also sometimes lose to The Queen's Lance.**
  Knight, pyro and reaper always win it, but reaper's wins run long (108-145s, brushing the cap).

These three bosses (Lance/pirate, Quartermaster, Tollmaster) are the strongest Phase-2 candidates
for a real difficulty fix — distinct from the Hornet Queen/Kings "too short" problem the brief
flagged and from the lab-gap group above.

## Deadliest / easiest levels

`tools/curve.mjs`'s static index (threat + hazard + gap, unaffected by RNG) and the boss-lab's
dynamic damage numbers agree well:

- **Deadliest by static index:** causeway (141), deep (139), hurricane (135), lamplit (129),
  fields (128) — all also show up as the hardest or longest boss fights above (causeway/kraken,
  hurricane/captain's huge taken/min, lamplit/tollmaster's outright failures, fields/strawking's
  partial failures). Good cross-check between the two independent methods.
- **Easiest by static index:** wood (66), marsh (70), hanging (97) — matches wood/queen and
  marsh/frog both being fast, low-index fights (though marsh/frog still spikes damage for
  paladin/reaper, see above).
- **King Gorm's own level (kings, index 120) is mid-high**, but the boss fight itself is trivial
  (~20s, near-zero damage) — the level's difficulty is front-loaded into the court gauntlet before
  him, not the boss.

### The four running levels (playtest bot, 1 pass x 6 heroes)

- **Wood**: healthy across all 6 heroes — 100-104 hp at the end, 0-3 deaths, 7-8 findings (none
  severe).
- **Marsh**: bot only reaches 7-16% (`walked`) for every hero — this is the known "bot cannot
  swim/ride" limitation (marsh's own rule is "ride the punt, or drain and wade"), not a real
  difficulty signal. Not a reliable read; needs a human or a swim-aware bot.
- **Stockade**: bot only reaches 19% for every hero with almost no damage (1 hit) and 0 deaths —
  same shape as the boss-lab's Chief gap above (the bot stalls on a bell/gate mechanic). Also not
  a reliable read.
- **Fields (Hexed Fields): the deadliest of the four by the play bot** — worst ending HP (pyro
  29hp, pirate 54hp after 20-30 hits and 3-4 deaths each), vs. wood/stockade heroes finishing
  near-full. Consistent with fields also containing the strawking/ploughman fights that gave
  several heroes trouble above. (Note: Hexed Fields is being actively changed by another agent —
  re-check this after that work lands.)
