# Mother Cap encounter revision

The hollow grows from 31 to 47 tiles wide (496 to 752 pixels), with six permanent platforms in two mirrored three-step routes. Each step rises 32 pixels. Spring caps and the one-hit-per-opening heart remain.

Phase one adds an aimed three-spore volley, a floor-wide root surge that asks for a jump or platform, and an elevated spore sweep with safe ground and upper platforms. At half health a two-second transition introduces marked root columns and a ten-spore radial burst. Ground hazards warn for 1.15 seconds and last 0.55 seconds. No adds spawn.

The root knot moves among four ground anchors during its rest, holds still when ready, and has a diamond outline, STRIKE ROOT / ROOT RESTING labels and an off-screen direction indicator. Its light follows it. Opening the heart cancels pending zone hazards; the existing eight-second opening and ten-second post-hit rest are preserved.

Validation: ordinary-input climbs on both sides for all six classes; both attack rotations; warning damage exclusion and safe lanes; phase-transition protection; four reachable knot anchors and light movement. Normal-health automated attack-only pilots: Paladin cleared at 105.4 seconds with 8 HP; the other five died with 13–38% boss health remaining. These pilots do not dodge the new patterns or use the new platforms, so these results are not human difficulty proof. Human/co-op playtesting and tuned defensive pilots remain useful.
