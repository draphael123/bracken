# Tower finish, 19 September 2026

Mage Tower uses continuous stone skins, arcane platforms and fixed structural ribs/windows. Interior campfire/garden/cairn clutter and the two nonboss floor acid vats are removed; raised spitting retorts retain their warning. Counterweights stay open eight seconds, count down once per second, and wait for either co-op player to clear the gate.

The Archmage remains the final Mage Tower boss and can die in the last inverted room. His Familiar is a separate miniboss in the new Falling Tower stage. Four floors visibly warn before collapsing onto safe lower routes, with return chains, three silver rewards, checkpoints, original music and an exit gated by the Familiar. Its guardable claw and spit and jumpable red slam each expose its eye.

Targeted tests cover reachability, gate obstruction, co-op gate clearance, wizard phase completion without transformation, all four collapses, all three Familiar attack transitions, defeat and stage exit. Forced damage is used to verify boss state transitions; these tests do not establish natural combat timing or six-hero balance.
