# THE KNIGHT REWORK — a brief

**Status: APPROVED BY DANIEL, 2026-09-23.** Queue position: after lane E's pose pass (both touch his combat code),
before the levelling system and the Geomancer.

## Why
Daniel: *the Warden is a lot more fun than the Knight.* The diagnosis: every Warden swing is a DECISION that pays off
visibly (tip/middle/haft by distance, the ring on a tip hit); her defence is an ACTIVE read (tap on the beat, yellow
only); her moves change the situation (pin, spit, skewer, floor); and her kit is built on openings you cause (A11).
Most Knight swings do the same thing wherever he stands, his default defence is holding C, and his kit grew by adding
charges rather than decisions. The Heavy Cut (live) was the first fix and Daniel liked it; this continues that line.
**Keep him a Knight:** sword, shield, stands his ground. Make standing his ground a skill.

## The four changes
1. **THE PERFECT GUARD BECOMES THE STAR.** It exists (a shield RAISED just as a blow lands: 6 frames, no stamina,
   attacker reels) but nothing teaches or rewards it. Give it a bright flash, a distinct clang, and a short
   **RIPOSTE WINDOW**: the next cut within ~0.6 s is guaranteed heavy. Teach it on the first level (a sign + a slow
   foe to practise on). This is his version of her deflect. (The PERFECT GUARD passive still doubles the window;
   COUNTERSTROKE still strikes back by itself.)
2. **THE THIRD CUT PAYS FOR WHERE YOU FINISH.** It already throws a foe bodily. A third cut that sends a foe INTO a
   wall, spikes, a pit or another foe gets a big payoff with its own feedback (impact, sound, number) — his version of
   the tip hit's ring. "Where do I finish the combo?" becomes his decision.
3. **BLOCKING IS A CHOICE, NOT A DEFAULT.** Holding the shield drains stamina steadily; heavy blows push him back
   (red still can't be blocked). Timing a guard should beat turtling every time. (STEADY ARM, the passive that made
   holding free, needs rethinking: e.g. "holding costs half".)
4. **THE HEAVY CUT STAYS** as it is live — the decision to drop the shield to hit hard.

## Done means
- Checks proving each: a perfect guard opens the riposte window and a late/early raise does not; a third cut into a
  wall/spikes/foe pays the bonus and one into open air does not; holding drains stamina and a timed guard costs none.
- The Knight's boss pilots re-run; nothing he could beat becomes unbeatable for the bot without a named reason.
- Daniel plays it and says it is closer to the Warden. That is the real test.
