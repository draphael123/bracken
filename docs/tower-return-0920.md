# The Falling Tower: return through the ruins

Rebuilt the former 350-tile course from the actual Mage's Folly builder, retaining its 712-tile width, rooms and masonry. The player starts in the old study and travels left through the observatory, fallen inverted halls, orrery, alchemy lab and library to the ruined entrance. Added reverse-facing steps, an archive crossing, orrery descent, warned falling masonry supported from the ceiling, four collapsing floors with lower refuges and escape chains, checkpoints and three silver coins. Old transformation glyphs and locked counterweights no longer obstruct the return. The original forward tower remains unchanged.

The Familiar remains the library mini-boss. Its art and hurt box are 70% of their former size (39 by 42 pixels). Its claw, slam and aimed spit are joined by a warned dash and three-shot spit fan. Each attack leaves the eye open. Its gate opens toward the left, and a completed mini remains completed after retry.

The Undead Archmage is the final boss. He uses a fire fan, low frost waves and a locked lightning column, with visible teleport destinations on surviving ground. He does not use the original mage's room transformations. At 75%, 50% and 25% health, outer slabs warn for three seconds and collapse, leaving an eleven-tile central platform. Spell casting pauses during collapse. Falls return the player to the centre with damage. Defeating him restores the exit bridge; death and retry restore the collapsing floors for a fresh attempt. Includes undead art, sound, bestiary, boss bar and boss-rush registration.
