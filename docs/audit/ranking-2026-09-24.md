# BRACKEN — every level and every boss, ranked (2026-09-24)

Measured against `origin/master` at `341cb78`, branch `claude/audit`. **No game code was changed.** STORMWRECK HARBOR is
shelved on purpose and is not ranked (its row is listed once, for the curve, and marked).

## Read this first — two things that are not rankings

1. **A CRASH IN THE FINALE.** The first full boss-lab pass over THE FALLING TOWER threw
   `TypeError: Cannot create property 't' on number '6' at updateUndeadMage (src/undead-mage.js:71)` and the page stopped.
   Cause, read in the code: the Death Knight's `markFoe()` (`src/main.js:5972-5975`) writes `e.mark = 6` on whatever it
   marks - including a boss, from any heavy blow (`src/main.js:7386`), the Harvest Moon (`:590`), the scythe throw and
   others. The Undead Archmage uses the SAME field for his death mark object: `if (e.mark) { e.mark.t -= dt; ...}`
   (`src/undead-mage.js:71`). A number 6 is truthy, and writing a property on it in a module throws every frame.
   **A Death Knight who lands a heavy on the last boss of the game crashes it.** The seeded reaper-only rerun happened not to
   land one and finished (56s), so this is not a flaky lab - it is a field-name collision. Fix: rename one of the two
   (`e.dkMark` for the Death Knight's) - `markFoe`, the tick at `:6053`, the reads at `:5372/:5470/:5965/:5968/:6330/:18105/:22371`.
2. **THE GREAT HOUND has no `mini: true` on his level entity** (`kings`: `{t:'greathound',x:230,y:13}`), so `bossLab({mini:true})`
   skips him ("no boss"). He was measured by answering `mini` for greathounds for that one row only (`tools/audit-bosslab.mjs`).
   Whether the game itself treats him as a mini everywhere it should is worth a look; this audit did not chase it.

Raw output is in `work/audit/` (bosslab.json, bosses-static.json, levels.json, pacing.json, judge-g1..g4.json, boss-table.md).

"dmg %hp/min" is the damage the bot took per minute as a percentage of its own max health, with health refilled so the fight
runs to its end: it is the **danger** number. "caused x N" counts how many times, summed over six heroes, the fight entered
the mode the player is supposed to cause - **x0 means the bot won without ever using the mechanic.**

A12 (heights): every room passes `tools/arena-supplies.mjs`. The Paladin's RADIANCE reaching all 110px of his yard is the

### Top five bosses
1. **THE PALADIN** (9) - the only window is the one your parry or dodge opens; phase two adds two unblockables.
2. **THE QUEEN'S LANCE** (8) - dash into his guard; at half he throws the lance away and is a different fight.
3. **THE FALSE ABBOT** (8) - bring him under his own bell; at half he rings it himself and takes it from you.
4. **THE MOTHER CAP** (8) - strike the glowbud; two phases that each add a layer.
5. **THE SCARECROW KING** (8) - every opening is something you do to his field; three stages.

### Bottom five bosses, and the concrete fix
1. **THE LAMPREEVE (2).** Make the window his lamp's, not his: strike the lamp he is hooding and the flare blinds him (open,
   2x, ~2.5s); a lamp he reaches unstruck gives him nothing. Add `draw` to `windingUp()` (main.js:18448). Phase two: with one
   lamp left he unhooks it and carries it, so the only light in the room moves.
2. **THE DIVING BELL (3).** Take `vent()` off the end of every attack (main.js:14411). Vent only when the player forces it:
   drop a ballast stone on the valve on his crown (the level already teaches ballast). Phase two: a new told attack, e.g. he
   sits on an air vent and starves it until knocked off, not just +speed.
3. **THE GREAT HOUND (3).** Make the opening his bestiary card promises worth taking: the blocked-lunge `skid`
   (main.js:13058) gets a 2x multiplier of its own and base damage outside it drops to ~0.6x, so a jumped lunge opens
   nothing and a blocked one is the fight. Add a fourth
   told attack (a told pack-call where the howl is now). Put `mini: true` on his `kings` entity.
4. **THE WEAVER (3).** Stop grounding her after every drop. Cut her thread during `dropTell` and she falls and lies open;
   a drop left alone climbs back up. Add a fourth told attack (a web snare across the floor). Her room is 55 wide - bring it to ~40.
5. **THE HEADLESS PLOUGHMAN (3).** `stuck` after every charge (main.js:10015) -> stuck only when the charge is baited into a
   fence post or furrow stone; a charge on open ground turns and comes back. Add a fourth told attack.

Next in line at 3: THE HOMUNCULUS (waited `spent` window - make a trick that misses you break his flask), THE GRANDMOTHER
(no gate at all), THE HEDGE WARDEN (harmless, 3 attacks).

**Also, regardless of rank:** the three silent windups - the Hornet Queen's `volleyUp`/`sweepStart`, the Chieftain's bow `aim`,
the Herald's `raise` - are one-line A2 fixes in `windingUp()`; and THE WINCHMASTER's drum jam should be the fight, not a bonus
on a boss you can simply hit.

# PART FOUR — WHAT I COULD NOT MEASURE, AND WHY

- **How a player does.** Every boss number is the lab bot's. Where the bot cannot play a mechanic, its number is not the
  fight's: THE KRAKEN (1/6 kills - its arms-and-stages fight is beyond the pilot; its difficulty is **not measured**), THE
  GOLEM (3/6, timeouts), THE TIDE HERALD (4/6), THE UNDEAD ARCHMAGE (4/6), THE MOTHER CAP (knight timed out). Where the bot
  won without the caused mechanic (THE WINCHMASTER downed x0, THE BURIED DEAD stuck x1, THE PYROMANCER overheat x3) that is a
  finding about the fight - the mechanic is skippable - only because the code confirms he takes full damage without it.
- **Refill health.** Fights ran with health refilled so they reach an end; win rate here is not a one-life win rate.
  `healthMode: 'normal'` was not run.
- **A10 and A11 are judgements.** They were read out of the code (four reading passes, citations in `work/audit/judge-g*.json`)
  and cross-checked against the lab's mode ledger; they are not a check. One reading pass read the wrong creature for THE
  KEEPER OF THE VAULT (the plain `bellguard`, not `e.vaultKeeper` -> `src/vault-keeper.js`); that row was re-read by hand.
  `tools/audit-bosses.mjs` has the same blind spot: a boss dispatched on a flag rather than on `e.t` is read as its base kind.
- **A1 counts** are "told attacks that do damage". A windup that is not a mode (a timer with no mode change) is invisible to
  the static read; the reading passes looked for those by hand.
- **A12** is heights only (arena-supplies.mjs). Distances, walls, hazards and props are not read by any tool.
- **F2 places, F6 distinct neighbours, B9, C1-C5 legibility, and the look** need eyes. The F8 sentence is the level's own
  `rule` line. "Sections" is only what a level declares in its data; most declare none.
- **F5** is read from set pieces and the rule line; whether each machine really "answers to the player and changes the room"
  was not verified in play.
- **THE UNBURIED FIELD** has no art, so it is ranked on its build only.
- **STORMHOLD's held rebuild** (`claude/stormhold`) and every other unmerged branch were not measured - master only.
- **The threat table itself.** INDEX is only as good as `src/threat.js`; `tools/threat-holes.mjs` says it is complete today, and
  the four bosses once written 0 there (closedhelm, bellcrab, drownedking, prince) are 6 today, so they count.
